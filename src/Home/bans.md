# Vinegar and (possibly) Sober Wrongful Terminations

![ban image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/1359283820771283154/1359298828494438583/Screenshot_20250408_183500.png?ex=67f6f92f&is=67f5a7af&hm=ead7233e43aaa58cf79ccead56f6694c6882c0e50086fa6ce4d1cc1e3ce4a526&)

## What is happening?

Linux players/developers are being wrongfully terminated (see the above ban image). It is believed that the bans are caused by something to do with Vinegar and Wine (the compatibility layer that is used as part of Vinegar), but there have also been reports, although a small number, of players being terminated without having ever used Vinegar and only having used Sober. The legitimacy of these claims are unknown. The underlying cause is still unknown as of writing this (8<sup>th</sup> April, 11:30PM UTC).

## What do we recommend?

For the safety of your account, **please refrain from using Vinegar**, or at the very least refrain from publishing via Vinegar. It would also be a good idea to **stay off Sober until more information is known**.

## I have been banned, what do I do?

Please **immediately [appeal your ban](https://www.roblox.com/support)**, detailing what has happened and why you are innocent. After your appeal is filed, it would also be appreciated if you could contribute to the ongoing investigation in the [VinegarHQ discord server](https://discord.gg/dzdzZ6Pps2). There is not much more than this that you can do other than wait for new developments.
